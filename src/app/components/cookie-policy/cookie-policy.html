Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to the site owners.

2. How We Use Cookies

Ultimate Ninja Storm Wiki uses minimal cookies and local storage technologies to ensure the basic functionality of the platform:

  • Essential / Technical Storage: We use your browser's localStorage to remember your choice regarding cookie consent (saving whether you clicked "Accept All" or "Reject Non-Essential"). This prevents the banner from reappearing on every page load.
  • Analytics & Performance (Optional): If enabled, we may use standard tools to analyze general traffic patterns and improve user experience on the wiki.

3. Managing and Deleting Cookies

You can control and/or delete cookies as you wish. You can clear all cookies already on your computer and set most browsers to prevent them from being placed.

You can also reopen our cookie preferences banner at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" button located in the footer of our website and changing your preferences.

4. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or site functionality. Please check this page periodically for updates.
