2. Data We Collect

As an informational fan wiki, we do not require user registration or collect personally identifiable information (such as your real name, email address, or phone number) for general browsing.

However, when you choose to use our community and matchmaking features by logging in via Discord OAuth2, we collect and process basic public profile information authorized through the identify scope, specifically:

  • Discord ID (unique user identifier)

We do not store your Discord password or access any private account data. This information is used exclusively to authenticate your session and manage community features.

Additionally, we utilize browser localStorage solely to save your cookie consent preferences (e.g., whether you accepted or rejected non-essential tracking). This data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.
